Help & Support Guide

Frequently asked questions and instructions for AI Learning Companion.

🤖 How does the AI Chat work?

Type your question in the chat box. The assistant searches its educational knowledge base to provide clear explanations for CS, AI, Math, and Science topics.

🌐 Can I ask questions in Hindi or Hinglish?

Yes! AI Tutor supports mixed-language queries. You can configure your AI response language preference in Settings.

🧪 How are Quiz Scores tracked?

Every time you complete a quiz session, your score is logged automatically and updated on your Progress Dashboard.

⚙️ How do I update my profile or language settings?

Go to Settings from the sidebar or click your user avatar in the top header to edit your profile and language options.
